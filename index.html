( WHO WE ARE )

Blckdot Studio was founded by renowned graphic designer Ulas Yesilyurt. With his passion for artistic expression. Ulas leads a world-class team specializing in different tattoo styles, based in Warsaw, Plac Jana Pawła II, Poland.

WHAT WE OFFER:

01.

Free Consultation

Our team is here to guide you, answer all your questions, and help you feel confident. Not sure about the design, placement, or style? Message us for a free consult.

02.

Custom Design

Every piece is uniquely designed for you. Share your vision or rough ideas, and our resident artists will transform them into an exclusive, custom tattoo masterpiece.

03.

Cover-Up & Rework

Transform old or unwanted tattoos into something you love. Our specialists use advanced blending and composition techniques to seamlessly conceal or upgrade your previous ink.

04.

Aftercare & Healing

Proper healing is essential for longevity. We provide comprehensive professional aftercare instructions, recommended healing products, and a follow-up check to ensure safety.

05.

Private Sessions

Experience maximum comfort and privacy in our dedicated VIP studio rooms. Perfect for large-scale projects, full-day sessions, or clients preferring an exclusive environment.
